Five context windows 8,16,32,64,128 in cell-image pixels for cell and tissue maps. Each window is divided into 4x4 bins; each bin is averaged densely. learned and AE encode the 10 cell channels into 16 dimensions and the 5 aligned tissue channels into 16 dimensions per scale. Concatenation has 15 raw channels plus 160 context channels. Fixed uses the BC,TC,CA ensemble mean within each spatial bin, giving 240 fixed context channels plus 15 raw channels. Context vectors are calculated at 64x64 locations (stride 16) and interpolated to 1024x1024. Raw 15 channels retain their original resolution. This is a spatial sampling approximation, not a separate full-resolution patch encoder at all 1048576 pixels. AE reconstructs the pooled 4x4 patch representations during pretraining, then encoders are fine-tuned with the supervised gate loss. Tissue source maps were cached at 256 resolution and aligned to the cell view, so small tissue windows are limited by that source resolution. All use identical D4 base predictions and bounded temperature softmax. Validation chooses the final method and postprocessing before test evaluation. Prior test exposure must be disclosed when reporting exploratory results.
